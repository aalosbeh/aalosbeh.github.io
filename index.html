Biography

About

Dr. Anas M. R. AlSobeh is an Assistant Professor of Applied AI in the Department of Information Systems & Technology at Utah Valley University (UVU). He previously served as an Assistant Professor and Program Coordinator of Information Technology & Cybersecurity at Southern Illinois University Carbondale (R1), and brings more than ten years of academic and research experience across the United States, Jordan, and Bahrain. He earned a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Utah State University and holds M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees in Computer Information Systems from Yarmouk University.

His research spans AI-driven cybersecurity, trustworthy and explainable AI, software engineering, cloud computing, and data science, with applications in malware and adversarial robustness, media forensics, and public-interest systems. He is Principal Investigator on a USDA-funded project (2024–2026) on rapid Salmonella detection in onions using microscopic imaging and AI, and on a 2026 Perkins V grant to build an Applied AI Incubator at UVU.

He has published 50+ peer-reviewed papers in leading venues (Q1/Q2) and received the IACIS 2024 Best Paper Award for work on deepfake detection and the Best Paper Award at IEEE SaTC 2026. He has contributed to and led international initiatives funded by EU Horizon 2020, Erasmus+, GIZ, DAAD, and Nuffic in refugee education, health and public-interest data systems, and cybersecurity capacity building, and has served as an expert consultant with ICMPD on AI and statistical analysis in migration contexts.

Dr. AlSobeh is an IEEE Senior Member and an ACM member, and an active mentor and reviewer with service roles across major journals and conferences. His training includes NVIDIA’s Building Transformer-Based NLP Applications, AWS Cloud Practitioner, and MIT’s Data Science and Machine Learning program.

Focus areas

Research Areas

Interdisciplinary work at the intersection of cybersecurity, trustworthy AI, software engineering, and scalable computing, grounded in applications that serve the public interest.

01

AI-Driven Cybersecurity

Malware and intrusion detection, adversarial robustness, prompt-injection defenses, exploit prediction, and agentic-AI security.

Selected work. Android malware detection (Cluster Computing, 2024); CodeLLM-Auth (COMPSAC 2026); PoC2Defense (CYBER-AI 2026).

02

Trustworthy & Explainable AI

Explainable, fair, and compliant AI for high-stakes decisions, including federated learning and counterfactual reasoning.

Selected work. AspectFL (Information, 2025); XAIPath (ICPP 2025); tax-fraud detection with XAI (IACIS 2025).

03

Software Engineering & AOP

Aspect-oriented programming, model checking, runtime monitoring, and transaction-aware software design.

Selected work. BlockASP (IEEE Access, 2023); OSM (OJCMT, 2023); AI-Powered AOP (IJACSA, 2024).

04

High-Performance Computing & Food Safety

HPC-enabled AI/ML pipelines for microscopy-based pathogen detection and scalable scientific workflows.

Selected work. USDA-NIFA Salmonella detection project; Journal of Food Protection (2026); BactoDetect.

05

Computer Vision & Media Forensics

Deepfake detection, microscopic image analysis, aerial change detection, and image immunization against diffusion-based editing.

Selected work. IACIS 2024 Best Paper (deepfakes); ImageArmor (IJCNN 2026); YOLOv8 microcolony detection (Applied Sciences, 2026).

06

NLP & Large Language Models

Domain-adapted LLMs, Arabic NLP, sentiment and radicalization detection, and LLM evaluation.

Selected work. SiAraSent (BDCC, 2026); RADAR# (Information, 2025); TAMUSA-Chat (arXiv, 2026).

07

Cloud, IoT & Distributed Systems

Secure cloud-native and IoT architectures, blockchain frameworks, and distributed transaction reliability.

Selected work. Cloud-based IoT water distribution framework; secure fog-based health monitoring (ICCNS 2024); TransJ.

08

Public-Interest & Education Systems

E-health frameworks, refugee education and empowerment, and AI-supported teaching and workforce training.

Selected work. Student-in-the-loop AI framework (SIGITE 2023); RAISD, NeuCARE, and LSFR projects; Perkins V Applied AI Incubator.
